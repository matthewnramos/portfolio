RateMyProfessor Department Rating Analysis

Python, Pandas, Jupyter

  • Processed 1.35M+ professor reviews into 10,686 professor-period observations across 40 universities, using Python and pandas to analyze department-level rating differences.
  • Applied multiple linear regression and stratified Kruskal-Wallis tests, finding that STEM professors rated 0.56 points lower than humanities professors after adjusting for review volume, school group and time period.
  • Analyzed rating differences across four university groups and COVID versus non-COVID periods, finding that department gaps persisted across most subgroups while COVID period alone was not a significant predictor.

BuildVCT - VALORANT Roster Draft & Tournament Simulator

TypeScript, Next.js, React, Tailwind

  • Built a data pipeline to scrape, clean and structure match and player statistics from 16 esports tournaments, covering 390+ matches and 1,000+ player stat lines.
  • Developed a seeded, map-by-map tournament simulation engine using ratings derived from historical match data, alongside a Redis-backed daily leaderboard.
  • Deployed an interactive roster-drafting application attracting thousands of viewers and reaching 200 concurrent users at peak.
